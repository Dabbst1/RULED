Questions

Do I need to download anything?

No. Monerem runs in your browser on any phone, tablet, or computer. Log in and start.

Do I need spreadsheet experience?

No. You type numbers into labeled boxes, and the math is done for you.

Can I cancel?

Yes, at any time from your account page. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for.

What happens to my numbers if I cancel?

They stay saved in your account. The tools lock when your membership ends, and you pick up where you left off if you come back.
